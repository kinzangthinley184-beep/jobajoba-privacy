Delete your JobaJoba account

Last updated: September 28, 2026

In the app

Open JobaJoba, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm twice. Your account is deleted straight away and can't be recovered.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, email jobajobaapp@gmail.com from the recovery email on your account, or include the phone number you signed up with. We'll confirm it's your account and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your name, phone number, email address, profile photo, task photos and identity documents (ID photo and selfie). Any open tasks and offers are cancelled, and anyone you were working with is told.

What is kept

Messages and reviews you left stay visible to the other person, shown as "deleted user" with no name, phone number or photo, because they are their record too. Messages are removed about 3 weeks after their task closes.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
