Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

JobaJoba is a task marketplace app connecting people in Bhutan who need help with a task to people willing to do it. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect

Your phone number, name, and (if you add one) a profile photo. Photos you attach to a task you post. Your government ID photo and a selfie, if you complete identity verification. Your device's location, to show you nearby tasks and suggest where you're posting from. The tasks, offers, messages, and ratings you create while using JobaJoba, and who you choose to block.

2. How we use it

To run the marketplace — showing you nearby tasks, connecting posters with workers, confirming who you are, and calculating ratings. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Location

We read your device's location live, when the app needs it, to show distances to nearby tasks and suggest your dzongkhag when you post one. We don't track or store a history of everywhere you've been — only the location a task itself is posted at, which is set by whoever posts it.

4. Verification documents

ID and selfie photos submitted for verification are stored in a restricted, encrypted-at-rest location and used only to confirm your identity. They are never shown to other users, and are not used for anything beyond verification.

5. Who can see your information

Your name, profile photo, and rating are visible to other users on tasks and in chat. Your phone number is never shown to other users — we use it only to sign you in. Payment for a task happens directly between you and the other person, in cash; JobaJoba doesn't process or store any payment or financial information.

6. Service providers

We use Supabase to host our database, file storage, and sign-in system, and Twilio to deliver the SMS code you use to sign in. Both only process your data on our behalf, to make JobaJoba work.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time from Settings inside the app. This permanently blocks sign-in and removes your name, phone number, and photo. Tasks, messages, and ratings tied to your account stay in place, without your personal details, so the history other users relied on doesn't break.

8. Who this app is for

JobaJoba is for people 18 and older — identity verification requires a government ID, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
