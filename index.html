Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

JobaJoba is an app connecting people in Bhutan who need a task done with people willing to do it. This is the same text shown in the app.

1. What we collect

Your phone number, name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash). A profile photo if you add one. The tasks, offers, messages, photos, ratings and reports you create, and who you block. If you verify your identity: a photo of your CID or passport and a selfie. If the app crashes: a crash report with your device model and system version.

2. How we use it

To run JobaJoba: signing you in, showing tasks, connecting posters with workers, confirming who you are, calculating ratings, keeping the community safe, and fixing crashes. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Location

Your phone's location is used on your phone to work out how far tasks are and to suggest your dzongkhag. It is not sent to us. A task you post is stored with its dzongkhag and the description of the place you type in.

4. Who can see what

Other users can see your name, photo, verification badge, rating and reviews. Your phone number, email and ID documents are never shown to other users. Messages are seen only by the two people in the conversation — except that when someone reports a problem on a task, our moderators can read the messages on that task to deal with the report.

5. Your ID documents

Your ID photo and selfie are kept in private storage and seen only by our reviewers. They are deleted 30 days after we approve or reject them, and immediately if you delete your account. The copies on your phone are deleted as soon as they are uploaded.

6. How long we keep things

Messages are deleted about 3 weeks after their task is closed. Declined offers are deleted after 30 days. Everything else is kept while your account exists.

7. Services we use

Supabase stores our database, files and sign-in. Sentry receives crash reports. Geoapify draws the maps, so it receives the location of tasks you look at and your internet address. They process data only to provide their service to us.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time from Settings. We delete your name, phone number, email, photos and ID documents, and cancel your open tasks and offers. Messages and reviews you left stay visible to the other person, shown as "deleted user", because they are their record too.

9. Who JobaJoba is for

JobaJoba is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you think we have, tell us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.

11. Contact

To ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted: jobajobaapp@gmail.com.
